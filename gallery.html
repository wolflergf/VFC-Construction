Privacy & cookies

This website does not store enquiry form entries or submit them to a server. The form prepares an email in your own email app; you choose whether to send it. VFC uses information you send to discuss your project and respond to your enquiry.

Contact Vaildo Castro at vava_london@hotmail.com to ask about your information, request correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern. You can also contact the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

This site stores only your cookie preference in localStorage. No analytics or advertising scripts are installed. Fonts, icons and Tailwind load from external providers, which receive basic connection information such as your IP address. WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps have their own privacy policies when you follow their links.

Project data retention, the legal business identity and any future form processor must be confirmed before public launch. This is the privacy notice for the current preview.
